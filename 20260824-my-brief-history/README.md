# my brief history

No one really asked, and no one needs to know, but I'm writing this because it is myself who often 
wonders how I ended up here. Not that I'm in pain or grief or even something remotely resembling a 
feeling of remorse, I'm just simply trying to understand my somewhat peculiar trajectory of life.

This life began with a father, a mother as usual for all human beings and stays as usual for a good
while until the father was destroyed by a disease called pulmonary fibrosis. It's a pretty terrible
one to be diagnosed with because the patient experiences a long period of slow, painful decline in
the ability to breathe and the dies a sudden death due to the heart failure.
